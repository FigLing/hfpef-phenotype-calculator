Incident HFpEF Phenotype Calculator

Assigns a patient to one of five prognostically distinct HFpEF phenogroups from 10 routine clinical variables. Derived and cross-validated in a 3-hospital Singapore cohort (N=3,772). Research/screening adjunct — not a diagnostic tool.

Important caveats
  • Screening/research aid, not a diagnostic label. Precision is moderate even where discrimination (AUC) is good.
  • Derived and validated only within a single 3-hospital Singapore cohort — not externally validated.
  • Requires eGFR, echo LV mass index, relative wall thickness (RWT), BMI, haemoglobin, E/e', age, sex, stroke/COPD history.
  • When the top probability is below 60%, treat the assignment as indeterminate and use clinical judgement.
